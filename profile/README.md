<p align="center">Software for schools, families, and social protection programmes.</p>

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Scolah** | Keeps families and schools connected: daily updates, attendance, activities and media. |
| **Scolah Compass** | Social registry and case management for NGOs and governments. |
| **Scolah Compass Resources** | Budget and stock management alongside Scolah Compass. |
| **Scolah Compass Exchange** | Moves data between Scolah Compass and the systems around it. |
| **Scolah Compass Platform** | Runs Scolah Compass on your own servers. |
